# ADR-010: Structural understanding safeguards and ceilings

Status: Accepted for Phase 2 implementation.

Meaning-level extraction, decomposition and structural criticism use the abstract
LLMProvider with versioned rubrics. Deterministic checks require extractive material
grounding, complete candidate-resolution records, graph integrity, preservation of
directed contribution relationships/features and separate meaningful combinations.
Six critic results are mandatory; unknown never counts as pass, and a model cannot
weaken a failed structural check by assigning INFO severity.

Exact feature/relationship/material-scope identity can align independent proposals.
Unresolved terminology may use explicit semantic feature correspondences only if
they form a bijection preserving directed predicates and material scope. Similarity
or graph topology alone never establishes equivalence. Ambiguous mappings remain
unresolved. Explicitly grounded split/merge repairs may resolve granularity; opposing
or unresolved interpretations remain visible even when the critic proposes consensus.

Material unresolved disagreement caps affected contributions at EXPLORATORY. The
aggregate sufficiency uses the lowest ceiling, retaining assessable dimensions and
affected IDs rather than inventing a verdict. This conservative ordinal policy is not
a novelty threshold or calibrated score. Missing mechanism caps comparison, while
missing relationship detail/critical blockers prevents HIGH_RESOLUTION. No identifiable
contribution yields INSUFFICIENT even when a landscape can be discussed.

New Phase 2 artifacts record resolutions, unknowns, versions and affected IDs.
Phase 1 contracts stay unchanged. An explicit context-only disposition may remove
non-mechanistic, non-relational context candidates; it cannot silently discard a
mechanism or relationship.

Cost: conservative grounding and aggregate ceilings may understate a well-specified
subset. Span/schema validation does not prove semantic entailment, critic reliability
or adversarial model robustness; default fixtures test orchestration and safeguards,
not a deployed model's general intelligence. Prior-art equivalence and novelty
adjudication remain deferred and must consume these limitations.
